Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Family Portal Backup is a private backup utility intended for personal use by its owner.

Google Drive access

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This allows it to create and manage backup files and access files explicitly made available to it. It does not request access to unrelated files in the user's Google Drive.

How information is used

Google Drive access is used solely to upload, identify, verify, download, restore, and manage encrypted backup files.

Storage and security

Backup contents are designed to be encrypted before upload to Google Drive. Access credentials are handled privately and are not included on this public website.

Sharing and retention

Google user data is not sold or used for advertising, profiling, or marketing. Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until removed by the owner or by an owner-configured retention process.

Public website

These informational pages do not request Google sign-in, upload documents, or collect personal information through forms.

Contact

Questions about this utility or its privacy practices can be submitted through the project contact page . Do not include personal documents or confidential information in public requests.
